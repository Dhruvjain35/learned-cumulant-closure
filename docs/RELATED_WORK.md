# Related work

Verified primary sources only (see [`../references.bib`](../references.bib)). Entries whose author/venue fields
are not fully confirmed are flagged in [`MANUSCRIPT_CLAIM_AUDIT.md`](MANUSCRIPT_CLAIM_AUDIT.md) and must be
confirmed before submission. This work claims a specific combination and a specific mechanistic finding; it does
**not** claim the individual ingredients are new.

**Summary of the honest position.** Cumulant truncations for open Tavis–Cummings superradiance are well studied,
the second-order burst-peak overshoot is already reported, as is the unphysical behaviour of odd truncation
orders, and learned closures and memory diagnostics exist. To our knowledge, we found no direct prior
demonstration of this exact combination in the evaluated disordered open-cavity setting: a frozen,
parameter-routed learned third-cumulant closure, benchmarked against the second-, third-, and fourth-order
truncations, with the burst-peak error attributed to two named connected third-cumulant channels. That is the
strongest wording the evidence supports, and a specialist literature check by a mentor is still required.
